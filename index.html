01 — GAME DESIGN & DEVELOPMENT

Flip Trail

In development

A route-planning puzzle where every step flips the board. Plan a path, spend your wire carefully, then run the route and see whether the logic holds.

How it works +

The win condition is simple: reach the finish within the wire budget and turn every coloured tile yellow. Revisits flip tiles again, so the shortest-looking route isn’t always the right one.
